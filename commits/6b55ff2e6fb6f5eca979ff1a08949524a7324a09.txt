fix(hypr): make shader switching idempotent and debounce the reload watcher
Hyprland destroys and recreates the screen shader program on every hl.config
call, even when the value is unchanged, so re-applying the active shader (which
the reload watcher did on every configreloaded) caused needless shader churn.
do_set now returns early when the target shader is already active (no
glslangValidator run, no grim check), and the watcher coalesces a burst of
reload events into at most one restore every 2 seconds.
