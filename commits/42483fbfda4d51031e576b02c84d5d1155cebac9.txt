feat(aether): theme GTK4/GTK3 from the palette
GTK was not part of the theme pipeline at all, and libadwaita ignores both
gtk-theme-name and gtk-application-prefer-dark-theme: it takes light/dark from
org.gnome.desktop.interface color-scheme, which nothing owned. A stale
prefer-light (left by nwg-look's "Color scheme" dropdown) therefore rendered
Nautilus white on a dark desktop, and xdg-desktop-portal reported the same value
to Chromium/Electron "follow system theme", so Brave saw a light system.

gtk4/gtk.css overrides the palette for GTK4/libadwaita apps in both dialects
(:root custom properties + @define-color); gtk3/gtk.css does the same for GTK3
on top of adw-gtk3. gtk4/post-apply.sh derives color-scheme from the palette
mode on every apply and switches remember-recent-files off (Nautilus shows its
Recent place only while GtkSettings' gtk-recent-files-enabled is true, and
settings.ini cannot override that mapped property); gtk3/post-apply.sh syncs
gtk-application-prefer-dark-theme in ~/.config/gtk-3.0/settings.ini, which wins
over gsettings for GTK3's dark variant.
