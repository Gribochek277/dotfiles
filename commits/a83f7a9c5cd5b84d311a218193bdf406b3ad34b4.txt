feat(silo): detach the NAS share around sleep and restore it
A live CIFS mount wedges suspend: once the network is down for sleep the
NAS is unreachable, and the kernel's pre-suspend filesystem sync
(pm_fs_sync -> ksys_sync -> sync_inodes_sb) blocks forever on the CIFS
writeback. The machine then neither sleeps nor wakes and has to be
hard-powered off (the 2026-10-08 suspend in the journal).

silo-sleep.service (Before=sleep.target, StopWhenUnneeded=yes) drives
root/silo-sleep:

  pre  - if ~/Silo is mounted, record it in /run and umount -l -f it;
  post - only if it was mounted before sleep, start the mount unit again.

umount -l detaches without contacting the server, so a dead NAS cannot
hang the hook. A share that was disconnected stays disconnected on
resume. install-root.sh installs the helper and enables the unit.
