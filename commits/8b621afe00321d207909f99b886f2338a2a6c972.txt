feat(nvim): pi model picker mirrors pi's available models at runtime
The picker now asks pi itself which models are usable (pi --list-models),
so it shows every provider with working credentials: freellmapi,
opencode-go, nvidia and the local llama.cpp instances (127 models).
Results are cached (mtime of models.json/models-store.json/auth.json +
10min TTL) and refreshed in the background, so consecutive picks are
instant. The default selection follows pi's own settings.json default
(opencode-go/deepseek-v4-flash) and still persists per pick across
restarts; the synchronous models.json parse remains as an offline
fallback.
