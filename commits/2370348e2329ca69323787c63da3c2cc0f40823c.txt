feat(aether): theme Qt/KDE apps (Kate editor theme, KDE colour scheme)
Qt has no colour scheme of its own: with no platform theme it falls back to its
built-in light palette, and kdeglobals is only honoured with plasma-integration
(not installed). That is why Kate opened light on this dark desktop, and why
qt6ct — which is installed — was no help: it refuses to apply palettes at all.

hypr/config/environment.lua now exports QT_QPA_PLATFORMTHEME=gtk3, so Qt and KDE
apps take their palette from the GTK3 theme Aether already generates. Measured
for a Qt6 app: window #261e21, text #ffeaea, highlight #94758e — the Aether
palette.

kde/ names the scheme ([General] ColorScheme=Aether) and rewrites the inline
[Colors:*] groups of ~/.config/kdeglobals from the rendered scheme: those groups,
not the scheme name, are what KColorScheme paints KDE chrome with
(plasma-apply-colorscheme does the same, but ships with plasma-workspace).

kate/ ships a KSyntaxHighlighting theme rendered from the palette (hues follow
the ANSI slots so editor and terminal agree, selection = accent like kitty) and
pins it in katerc, switching Auto Color Theme Selection off — with it on, Kate
substitutes Breeze Light/Dark instead.

The four post-apply scripts now append a dated status line to
theme-engine/<app>-post-apply.log, the per-app log the herdr/sddm scripts use.
