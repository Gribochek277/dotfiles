feat(silo): rofi menu, 4-state indicator, failure guard, sudo askpass
Silo (new stow target, ~/.config/silo):
- siloctl is the single entry point (state/auto-state/json/status/connect/
  reconnect/disconnect/auto-on/auto-off/open/reachable), also symlinked as
  ~/.local/bin/siloctl
- state comes from /proc/mounts + unit states, never from `mountpoint -q`
  on the autofs watchpoint (which always reports "mounted" and triggers a
  mount attempt just by looking at it)
- silo-guard: OnFailure/OnSuccess watchdog on home-serhii-Silo.mount that
  disables the automount after 3 consecutive failures, so a dead NAS stops
  being hammered by gvfsd-recent; counter lives in /run
- privileged verbs via sudo: plain in a terminal, `sudo -A` from waybar/rofi.
  Fingerprint first (pam_fprintd in /etc/pam.d/sudo), themed rofi askpass as
  the fallback, with a notification hint while PAM waits for the sensor
- install-root.sh installs both helpers, the guard units, the mount drop-in
  and /etc/pam.d/polkit-1 (fingerprint for the remaining polkit dialogs)

waybar custom/silo:
- 4 states connected/auto/failed/off with distinct colors
- left click opens the rofi menu, right click toggles connect/disconnect
- signal 31 for refreshes, interval 5s

aether (new stow target, ~/.config/aether/custom):
- mako template: notifications follow the active palette (accent border,
  red + sticky for critical) instead of the hand-written Dracula config
- stow --no-folding: Aether skips symlinked directories in custom/
